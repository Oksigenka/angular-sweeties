— OUR STORY

Baked with Passion,
Served with Love

Sweet Bliss was born in 2009 from a simple dream: to bring the warmth of homemade baking to every celebration. What started as a small kitchen operation has grown into a beloved local institution.

Our founder, Elena Rossi, trained in Paris and Lyon before returning home with a suitcase full of recipes and a heart full of inspiration.

We source our ingredients locally whenever possible — seasonal fruits, farm-fresh eggs, and single-origin chocolate.
